# Participant guide: author, co-author, listener

This guide is for everyone taking part in an event run on an OJS journal site: authors who submit abstracts or papers, co-authors, listeners, and parents or legal guardians of minor participants. It explains how to submit, confirm co-authorship, register as a listener, pay and get a certificate.

Button and menu names in your journal may be in English or Ukrainian depending on the site language.

## Before you start

- You need an account on the journal site. If you do not have one, click **Register** in the top menu of the OJS site.
- Look for the event information (dates, conditions, fees, venue) on the event page. The call for papers usually links to it. The **Upcoming events** page (`/index/conference/upcoming`) may list events of all journals of the site.
- Your participant cabinet is at `/{journal}/conference/my` and is called **My event participations**. Invoice letters and reminders also link to it.

## How to submit an abstract or a paper

1. Open the event page and click **Submit**. The button is there only while the call is actually open. You can also start a new submission in the journal in the usual way.
2. Go through the OJS submission wizard: upload the file and add all co-authors with correct email addresses.
3. At the **Conference participation** step (after "For the Editors"), fill in the speaker questionnaire:
   - **Event:** choose the event you are submitting to. Only events with an open call are shown, with their closing date. If the journal also accepts regular articles, you can choose "Not for a conference (regular article)" here.
   - **Preferred presentation type:** oral presentation, poster, online presentation or in absentia (publication only).
   - **Form of participation:** in person, online or in absentia.
   - **Presenter:** the presenter's email exactly as in the list of contributors.
   - **Participant category:** the fee depends on it. For some categories the organizers will ask for proof, such as a student card.
   - If the presenter is under 18, tick **The presenter is under 18** and give the **Parent or legal guardian consent** (see the section on minors).
   - **Consent to personal data processing:** read the text and tick **I agree to the terms of personal data processing**.
4. At the **Review** step, check the questionnaire answers. Errors are shown next to the fields.
5. Submit.

Some fields are filled in for you: with one open event it is already selected, you are the presenter, and the form of participation matches the event format.

### When the submission cannot be sent

- No event is chosen, or the call for the chosen event has already closed.
- The questionnaire is incomplete or no category is chosen.
- The presenter is not among the contributors: check the email in the list of contributors.
- There is no consent to personal data processing, or the consent text has changed and you need to agree again.
- A minor presenter has no consent from a parent or legal guardian.

### What happens after you submit

- All authors become participants of the event: the presenter with the role "presenter", the others as "co-author".
- Co-authors get a letter to confirm their participation.
- You get a letter after the editorial decision. If the paper is accepted, the letter names the presentation type. If participation is paid, the presenter is invoiced.
- The paper is published only after the invoice is paid (or waived).

## How to confirm co-authorship

If you are listed as a co-author, you get a letter "Please confirm your participation: …" with a link.

1. Open the link from the letter. You do not need an account for this.
2. Click **I confirm my participation** or **I decline**.

The link is valid for 30 days and works only once. If you see "The link has expired" or "The link is not valid or has already been used", contact the organizers of the event.

A co-author participation added earlier appears in your cabinet when you log in with the same email.

## How to register as a listener

1. Open the event page and click **Register as a listener**. The button is there while registration is open.
2. If you are not logged in, the site asks you to log in. Your name and email come from your account.
3. On the **Listener registration** page, fill in:
   - **Participant category**;
   - **Form of participation**;
   - **Affiliation** and **Position**;
   - if you are under 18, tick **I am under 18** and give the parent or legal guardian consent;
   - the consent to personal data processing.
4. Click **Register**.

After registering you land in your cabinet. If participation is paid, the invoice is there right away and a letter arrives by email. You can have only one active listener registration per event.

## The participant cabinet

Open `/{journal}/conference/my`. It shows all your participations in events of this journal, as a listener, presenter or co-author, with the role, category, form of participation, confirmation state and submission.

### How to pay an invoice

Under **My invoices**, each invoice shows the amount, the state, the **Payment reference**, the **Payment purpose**, the **Payment method**, the **Due date** and the instructions.

1. Click **Download the invoice (PDF)**.
2. Pay to the account in the invoice at your bank or in online banking. Enter the payment purpose exactly as in the invoice.
3. If the organizers take payment on an external page, click **Go to payment** and put the invoice number in the comment.

**Note:** a transfer to a card number is not a payment to the institution's account. Pay only to the IBAN in the invoice. There is no online card payment directly on the journal site.

### How to say that an organisation pays

If the journal uses the Ukrainian profile, an unpaid invoice has a **Payer details** section.

1. Under **Who pays the invoice**, choose **I personally** or **An organisation (institution, company)**.
2. For an organisation, enter the **Full name of the organisation** and its EDRPOU code.
3. If the organizer's accounting department requires it, enter the personal tax number.
4. Click **Save the details**.

The details go into this invoice while it is unpaid, and into later invoices. A paid invoice keeps its details.

### How to send a receipt

After paying, send a proof of payment so the organizers see it sooner.

1. Under the invoice, find **Send a receipt or payment order**.
2. Choose a file: PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB.
3. Click **Send**.

**Sent receipts** shows the state of each receipt: "Awaiting review", "Accepted" or "Rejected". If a receipt was rejected, the reason is under **Manager's note** and in the letter. Send the right receipt.

- The same file is not accepted twice for the same invoice.
- One invoice can have up to 5 receipts awaiting review and up to 20 in total.
- Files are stored privately: only you and the organizers can see them.

### How to download a certificate

When the organizers issue a document, it appears under **My certificates**.

- **Download PDF** gives the document in the language it was issued in.
- **Download (EN)** gives the English version when the document was issued in Ukrainian.
- **Verification page** opens the public page of the document.

A listener certificate is issued when the organizers mark your attendance. A speaker gets a certificate for an accepted paper and attendance, an absentee author after the paper is published. For online events, attendance is often taken from the meeting report: join the meeting with the same email as in your registration.

### How to download your data

Click **Download my data (JSON)**. You get a file with everything the plugin stores about you in this journal: questionnaires and participations, invoices, payments and receipts, certificates, letters and mailings.

To have your data removed, write to the organizers of the event: a journal manager performs anonymisation. Invoices and issued documents keep their copies of the data, because accounting and the register of documents require them.

### How to cancel your participation

Click **Cancel participation** next to the participation. The text next to the button says until when this is possible: until registration closes, or, if there is no such date, until the event ends. After that, contact the organizers.

- Unpaid invoices of this participation are cancelled.
- Paid invoices get the state "refund requested". The organizers arrange refunds by hand; get in touch with them.
- When a presenter cancels, the submission is not withdrawn. To withdraw the paper, write to the editors.

## Minor participants

If a participant is under 18:

- a listener ticks **I am under 18** when registering;
- an author ticks **The presenter is under 18** in the submission wizard.

Then the checkbox **A parent or legal guardian consents to participation and data processing** appears. Registration or submission cannot be completed without it. A parent or legal guardian should tick it, or the participant together with them. The plugin keeps only the minimum data: name, institution and contact. A minor listener's position is not stored and no date of birth is asked.

## How to verify a certificate

Anyone, for example an employer or another institution, can check whether a document is genuine.

1. Scan the QR code on the certificate. It leads to the **Certificate verification** page of the journal that issued it.
2. Or open `/{journal}/conference/verify`, enter the **Verification code** from the QR link and click **Check**.

The page shows whether the document is valid ("The certificate is valid: this journal issued it."), with the number, kind, recipient, event, institution, paper, hours and date of issue. A revoked document shows the reason. A replaced document shows the number of the new one. One address can make up to 30 checks a minute.

If the journal uses the Ukrainian profile, `/{journal}/conference/documents` is the public register of issued professional development certificates.

## The event program

When the organizers publish the program, the event page shows the link **Program of the event: papers by section**. The program lists accepted papers by section and presentation type. There is no schedule with rooms and times in it yet: the organizers may give details in the program introduction.
