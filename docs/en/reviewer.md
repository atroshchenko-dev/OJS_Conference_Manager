# Reviewer guide

This guide is for users with the **Reviewer** role in the journal that runs the event. The review itself happens with the usual OJS tools: you get a request, accept or decline it, read the files and fill in the review form. The plugin adds one thing: you can choose which submissions you would like to review.

## How to choose submissions to review

If the committee opened the choice, you get the email "Choose submissions to review" with a link. The same link is in your cabinet `/{journal}/conference/my` under **Choosing submissions to review**.

1. Open the link and sign in.
2. Look through the submissions: the title, section, keywords and abstract (click **Abstract** to expand it). Author names are not shown, and your own submissions are not listed.
3. For each submission choose:
   - **very keen**: the topic is very close to you;
   - **willing**: you are ready to take it;
   - **rather not**: better not to assign it to you;
   - **conflict of interest**: you know the authors, work with them, or have another interest that keeps you from reviewing impartially;
   - **not chosen**: no mark.
4. Click **Save my choice**.

You can change your choice any time until the date shown on the page. After it the page shows your choice without changes.

## What happens next

The committee gets a proposed distribution: each submission gets reviewers from those who chose "very keen" or "willing", taking into account how many open reviews each one has. If a submission is assigned to you, you get a "Review request" email with a link to the review page and the due dates, and a task appears in your OJS reviewer dashboard. Whether to accept the request is up to you, as always.

A "rather not" or "conflict of interest" mark means the plugin never proposes you for that submission. An editor can still assign a reviewer by hand, so if you get a request for a submission with a conflict of interest, decline it and explain why.
